**PREFACE**

Every day, every minute, your business generates a large amount of information.

* Employee conversations in chats
* Employee conversations with customers
* New entries in your CRM or ERP system
* New video material from your store or production site

The volume of such information is enormous and usually goes unprocessed.

With the advent of AI, analyzing this information has become easier. The **[5Scontrol](https://5Scontrol.com/)** system allows you to run your text prompts across all this data.

You can connect to standard information sources:

* CRM / ERP / any other software
* Cameras at your work site or store

The system will automatically, at a set interval, check the information that belongs to you and that your business depends on. After analyzing this information, you will have access to daily, weekly, and monthly reports in the form of clear charts. From a chart, you can navigate to any specific data point:

* A voice conversation with a customer
* A video clip where a worker is performing a specific work operation
* An entry in your CRM / ERP system


**MORE ABOUT VIDEO POSSIBILITIES**


**[5Scontrol](https://5Scontrol.com/)** (5S) is a video monitoring system with AI analysis and ERP Integration (Open Source) that helps companies **improve productivity**, **eliminate waste**, and **enhance workplace safety** using computer vision (CV) and machine learning (ML).

The platform **generates detailed reports** to provide insights on how processes can be optimized.

Plug in your ML-algorithms or some of the Official 5s algorithms and get a **ready-to-use software** for manufacturing monitoring.

<br>

For **absolutely free**, you can install 5Scontrol on your computer, connect **up to 10 cameras** and get access to:
- violation reports
- missing operation reports
- workplace load reports
- archive of video recordings
- integration with your ERP
